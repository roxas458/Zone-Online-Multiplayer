# ZONE ONLINE 4.33.24 — Map Faction Markers & Mutant Ecology

## Changes
- Tactical map NPC squad markers are clickable in both Zone and local map modes.
- Selecting a squad opens a field card with squad size, member names, roles, current state and health.
- Mutant markers are red, clickable and show species, group count, aggression and condition.
- Existing faction marker colors remain: friendly green, neutral yellow, hostile red, online players cyan.
- Mutant ecology migrates existing worlds from 18 packs to 54 persistent packs and starts new worlds with 54.
- Map selection cards are responsive on narrow/mobile screens.
