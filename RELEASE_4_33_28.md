# ZONE ONLINE 4.33.28 — Camp Life & Gradual Sector Travel

- Offline sector travel now follows the canonical inter-sector route graph and advances one sector at a time on a 25-second travel interval.
- The current location changes only when each route segment is reached; destination confirmation no longer teleports the player.
- Travel consumes expedition supplies and survival resources per reached sector.
- Sector travel can be cancelled through the existing movement cancel control.
- The left PDA navigation removes the separate Inventory entry; the backpack button in the top header remains the inventory entry point.
- The former Living Zone 4.2 navigation entry is renamed Camp Life. Its existing relationship, faction rank, contracts, patrols, fieldwork, survival, notes, campfire, rumors and shelter systems are retained in the new section, with sticky jump navigation for easier browsing.
- Online travel continues to use the server-authoritative expedition system.
