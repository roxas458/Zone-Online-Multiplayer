# ZONE ONLINE 4.33.0 — SURVIVAL & EXPEDITIONS

- Real-time hunger, thirst and stamina simulation with bounded catch-up.
- Expedition kit preparation consumes one ration and one water bottle.
- Sector travel consumes supplies and stamina; dangerous routes add radiation exposure.
- Added field camp, temporary shelter and mutant hunting.
- Online mutant hunting is server-authoritative and persists pack count and player rewards.
- Added mutant trophy material and market value.
- Added survival HUD and event history to the Living Zone panel.

Render commands: `yarn install` / `yarn start`.
